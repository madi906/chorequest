# ChoreQuest User Stories

## Epic: Household Management

### US-001

**As a Parent**, I want to create a household so that my family members can participate in the application.

---

### US-002

**As a Parent**, I want to invite family members so that everyone can access the same household.

---

## Epic: Chore Management

### US-003

**As a Parent**, I want to create reusable chore templates so that I do not have to recreate common chores every week.

---

### US-004

**As a Parent**, I want to assign chores to my children so that responsibilities are clearly communicated.

---

### US-005

**As a Child**, I want to see my assigned chores so that I know what needs to be completed.

---

### US-006

**As a Child**, I want to mark a chore as completed so that my parent can review it.

---

## Epic: Approval

### US-007

**As a Parent**, I want to approve completed chores so that points are awarded fairly.

---

### US-008

**As a Parent**, I want to reject a completed chore so that my child can improve the quality of their work.

---

## Epic: Points

### US-009

**As a Child**, I want to earn points after my chores are approved so that I stay motivated.

---

### US-010

**As a Parent**, I want to view each child's point balance so that I can monitor their progress.

---

## Epic: Rewards

### US-011

**As a Child**, I want to redeem my earned points for rewards so that I feel motivated to complete chores.

*(Planned for a future sprint.)*

---

## Epic: Reporting

### US-012

**As a Parent**, I want to view reports and trends so that I can understand my family's participation over time.

*(Planned for a future sprint.)*
